Our Methodology

The Three Scientific Pillars of Sarvam

How we transform raw wildcrafted botanicals into standardized medicine without stripping the plant's innate prana (vital force).

🔥
Pillar One

Authentic Shodhana (Purification)

Raw botanicals and minerals contain naturally occurring impurities. We adhere strictly to *Rasatarangini* protocols—processing Shilajit through seven Surya Tapi agni washes in Triphala decoctions and incinerating nano-gold across 108 Puta cycles.

🧪
Pillar Two

Supercritical Fluid CO2 Extraction

Conventional herbal powders degrade within weeks, while chemical alcohol extracts leave toxic traces. We extract whole-spectrum phytocompounds using pressurized CO2 at ambient temperatures, preserving heat-sensitive terpenes and flavonoids.

🔬
Pillar Three

HPLC & ICP-MS Molecular Fingerprinting

Every production lot is fingerprinted for clinical standardization: minimum 5.0% Withanolides in Ashwagandha, 65% Fulvic Acid in Shilajit, and 95% Curcuminoids in Haridra. Complete Certificate of Analysis (COA) is accessible by every patient.
